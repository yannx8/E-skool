- (direction) Topic: where E-skool goes next + what makes Douala schools adopt it. First time using BMad, starting from zero. Stance: Creative Partner
- (direction) Batch: Five Whys, Assumption Reversal, Disney Method, Starbursting
- (technique) started Five Whys
- (insight by user) Why#1: schools are used to Excel sheets, physical registers, manual work; their rough tools already do the job (incumbent = good enough)
- (insight by user) Why#2: switching costs more than it saves; re-keying every pupil+fee; paper is free and secretary already knows it (coach offered, user chose over 'paper is trusted/visible')
- (insight by user) Why#3: the saving is real but unseen losses, e.g. unpaid fees nobody chases (coach offered 3 options, user chose unseen losses)
- (insight by user) Why#4: all three are true: registers/Excel hold the data, nobody owns tracking who owes what (director trusts totals); chasing parents face to face is socially awkward so it is skipped; data is scattered in notebooks so nobody can add it up
- (insight by user) Why#5 ROOT: directors already know about the losses (awareness is not the problem, so a 'show them the number' pitch will not sell)
- (direction by user) Strategic focus: everything that touches money in a primary school, founder to pupils, through staff/director/HR/parents, for full transparency
- (idea by user) One view to monitor entire school finances: fees and moratoires across classes and pupils
- (idea by user) Automatic WhatsApp reminders to parents
- (idea by user) HR/wages manager can monitor and manage payroll
- (idea by user) Founder gets clear financial transparency over the whole school
- (idea by user) Director stays in order with parents on pupil fees via well-managed moratoires (grace periods before paying)
- (idea by user) Dedicated staff get the exact list of pupils owing, and monitor all parent/pupil debts to school
- (idea by user) Asset finances tracked too
- (idea by coach) Moratoire as a signed promise: parent commits a date via WhatsApp, system auto-flags a broken promise and escalates to director
- (technique) Five Whys complete; next Assumption Reversal
- (idea by user) Founder hook: 'Financial transparency across your schools' = multi-school consolidated finance view for owners with several schools
- (insight by coach) Multi-school shifts buyer from director to founder/group owner; current model is single school (no tenant above school)
- (decision by user) Out of scope for now: multi-tenant / multi-school. E-skool stays single-school
- (technique) started Assumption Reversal
- (direction by user) Assumption Reversal: chose to flip #2 (school pays), #3 (parents are passive targets), #4 (must replace paper/Excel entirely)
- (idea by coach) Flip#2: parents pay for it (small fee / per-term subscription) or it is free to the school and funded by a cut of recovered fees
- (idea by coach) Flip#3: parents are active users: pay from phone (Mobile Money), see balance, request their own moratoire, get receipt on WhatsApp
- (idea by coach) Flip#4: E-skool never replaces Excel/paper: secretary keeps the notebook, E-skool is a shadow ledger fed by photo of the register or Excel upload
- (decision by user) Rejected flip#3: parents are NOT active users for now, they only receive (WhatsApp) reminders. Users = director, founder, specialized staff, app administrator
- (direction by user) Scope: founder finance transparency + HR/payroll finance + pupil fees + school resources (purchased assets, consumables) + in-kind supplies parents must bring at registration (A4 sheets, chalk, toilet paper...) tracked per pupil
- (direction by user) Ask: research all financial aspects of a school; define target user roles from research, use cases and responsibilities
- (insight by coach) Research: Cameroon private primary school cost = enrollment fee + tuition tranches + APEE/PTA + FBS (school supplies fund) + insurance + in-kind supply list (notebooks, slate, chalk...). Tuition 150k-800k FCFA/yr, back-to-school total 250k-900k
- (insight by coach) Research: school finance areas = student receivables, payroll, budgeting/expense control, procurement, inventory, fixed assets, petty cash/cash book
- (idea by coach) In-kind ledger: per-class supply list (A4 ream, chalk box, toilet paper), per pupil expected vs received, shortfall can be converted to FCFA or carried; feeds school stock
- (insight by coach) Existing app roles are admin/owner/secretary only; brainstorm needs director, founder, HR/payroll officer, collections/bursar staff, store keeper, app admin
- (insight by coach) Roles proposed: Founder (read+approve, transparency), Director (rules: moratoires, discounts, approvals), Bursar/Accountant (cash, expenses, closing), Collections officer (owing list, reminders), HR/payroll officer, Store keeper (stock+in-kind), Secretary (intake+receipts), App admin (users, config, backup)
- (direction by user) Ask: competitor and market research: where competitors succeed/fail and why, bad user feedback, market size, what target users expect, how they operate today, what they would pay for and why
- (insight by coach) Competitors (Cameroon): SchoolMboa, Akademise, EduCam Pro, Kalara, Ubora Ecoles, Skoulou, EduManager Pro, Akademi; rails: Schooly/Skulpay, Ecobank EDUC, Campay/Monetbil. All lead with grades/report cards + MoMo/OM fee collection
- (insight by coach) Pricing seen: SchoolMboa 250k FCFA one-time (stock module +50k); EduCam Pro 16k/mo (200 pupils), 35k/mo (500); Kalara 500 FCFA/pupil/yr. Cannot win on price
- (insight by coach) Market: private = 26.9% of primary enrollment (~1.38M pupils); internet 43.9%; MoMo ~7M, OM ~5M users; no count of private primary schools found
- (insight by coach) Inferred gap (unverified, no user complaints found): founder-level finance transparency, moratoires as tracked promises, per-pupil in-kind ledger, payroll+expenses+fees in one view. Need 5-10 director interviews
- (decision by user) Roles undecided until tested in a real school; role model stays flexible
- (direction by coach) Proposed vertical: E-skool = school finance and accountability system for private primary schools in Douala (not a full school ERP). Wedge: moratoires + in-kind ledger + founder transparency, Excel/paper-compatible, WhatsApp-out only. Not grades/report cards, not multi-tenant, not parent app
