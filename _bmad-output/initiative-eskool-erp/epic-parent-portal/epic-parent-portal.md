# Parents follow their balance and report cards

## Description

The Parent portal (Family overview, Receipts and statement, Moratoire request and answer, Report cards), the promoter's Term report, Excel exports, Guardian consent and data requests (UJ-6, UJ-7). Release R4.

## Outcome

At least 40% of the pilot school's Families sign in to the portal in term 3 (SM-5).

## Done when

1. A Guardian with two children signs in with phone and code and sees only their Family, in under 3 s on a throttled 3G profile (FR-49).
2. A Guardian requests a Moratoire inside the rules and accepts a counter-proposal (FR-51).
3. The Term report figures equal the dashboard at the report date (FR-40).
4. Exports cover every row, and anonymising a Family keeps totals and the Audit log intact (FR-56, FR-57).

## Boundaries

`portal` screens over `fees` and `academics` queries; exports and consent live in `core`.
